Our Story

Lumina was proudly founded by Paris Botchway and supported by Maame Akua Peace Konadu Nyarko at just 12 years old. What began as a shared spark of imagination quickly evolved into a fully realized cosmic universe, driven by a deep passion for luxury minimalism and the mysteries of the night sky.

We believe that everyday items should be more than just functional—they should inspire you to look upward. Our designs pull direct inspiration from distant galaxies, map out complex stellar constellations, and reflect the calming, ethereal energy of the stars. Every single thread in our streetwear line, every tone in our cosmetic drops, and every line in our stationery sets are carefully considered to match this premium celestial aesthetic.

At Lumina, youth is our greatest strength. It gives us a completely fresh, bold perspective on design and style. Our mission is to build a modern community focused on self-care, creative expression, and unmatched elegance. Thank you for walking among the stars with us and choosing to explore your true, brilliant glow.
