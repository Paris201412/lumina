Our Story

Lumina was founded by Paris Botchway at just 12 years old, built on a vision that everyday items should inspire a sense of wonder and calm.

Drawing aesthetic vision from serene lavender dusk skies, clean cosmic lines, and minimalist design structures, we engineer high-end artifacts crossing fashion, utility, and self-care space.

We craft for the dreamers, creators, and bold spirits who demand both premium tactile luxury and soft, comforting warmth from the physical elements they surround themselves with.
